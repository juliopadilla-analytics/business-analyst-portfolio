## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee benefit enrollment and premium costs using Power BI and Excel.

The dashboard was designed to help management understand benefit plan enrollment, monthly and annual premium costs, employer contributions, employee contributions, and coverage tiers.

## Business Questions

- How many employees are enrolled in each benefit plan?
- What are the total monthly and annual premium costs?
- How are costs divided between employer and employee contributions?
- Which coverage tiers have the highest enrollment?
- How do benefit plans compare by cost and participation?

## Dashboard Features

- Total Plan Enrollment KPI
- Total Monthly Premium KPI
- Total Annual Premium KPI
- Enrollment by Plan Type
- Enrollment by Benefit Plan
- Coverage Tier Analysis
- Employer vs. Employee Contribution Analysis
- Interactive slicers for employee, plan type, benefit plan, and coverage tier
- Detailed matrix for benefit cost analysis

## Data Preparation

The original employee benefits data was cleaned and structured in Excel and Power Query before being loaded into Power BI.

Sensitive employee information was removed or replaced for portfolio use.
